One minute

Ask a creator you watch.

Creators read their comments, and a note from a real viewer lands differently than an email from a campaign. This is how most of the creators already on board first heard about it.

  1. Pick a creator you genuinely watch. It works better and it is more honest.
  2. Leave a comment in your own words. Short and friendly beats earnest and long.
  3. Mention teamhuman.org so they can look it up themselves.

A few that read like a person rather than a campaign:

“Unrelated to this video, but you’d probably have something worth saying about teamhuman.org. A lot of creators are signing.”
“Would love to hear your take on the AI slowdown thing. Kurzgesagt and a bunch of others signed at teamhuman.org.”
“You talk about this stuff more thoughtfully than most. teamhuman.org, if you haven’t seen it.”

Please do not paste the same line on twenty channels. Platforms treat that as spam and creators can tell instantly. One real comment on a channel you actually follow is worth more to us than fifty copies of the same sentence, and a campaign that looks astroturfed loses the argument before it starts.

Two minutes

Call your representative.

Staff count the calls. A handful on one subject in one week gets noticed in a way that a thousand signatures somewhere else does not. You do not need to know anything about AI policy.

  1. Find your representative’s office number below.
  2. Say you are a constituent, and give the town you live in.
  3. Read the sentence below, or say it in your own words.
  4. Say thank you and hang up. That is the whole call.
“Hi, I’m a constituent from [your town]. I’m calling because I want humans kept in control of AI. Please support an international agreement to slow down development of smarter-than-human AI until it can be built safely. Thank you.”

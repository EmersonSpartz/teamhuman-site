Ask a creator you watch.

    
Creators read their comments, and a note from a real viewer lands differently than an email from a campaign. It is how several of the creators already signed first heard about this.

    
    
      
  1. Pick a creator you genuinely watch. It works better and it is more honest.
    2. 
      
  2. Leave a comment in your own words. Short and friendly beats earnest and long.
    3. 
      
  3. Mention teamhuman.org so they can look it up themselves.
    4. 
    

    
A few that read like a person rather than a campaign:

    
“Unrelated to this video, but you’d probably have something worth saying about teamhuman.org. A lot of creators are signing.”

    
“Would love your take on the AI slowdown thing. Kurzgesagt and a bunch of others signed at teamhuman.org.”

    
“You cover this more thoughtfully than most. teamhuman.org, if you haven’t seen it.”

    
There is deliberately no copy button here. Twenty identical comments under one video reads as astroturfing, platforms treat it as spam, and creators spot it immediately. One real comment on a channel you actually follow is worth more than fifty copies of the same sentence.

Before you ask

    
Does contacting a representative actually do anything?
      
Offices tally what constituents raise. Staff report the counts, and volume on an unfamiliar subject is what moves it up the list. You are not trying to win an argument, you are adding one to a tally that someone reads.

    
Do I need to know anything about AI?
      
No. Say you are a constituent, say you want humans kept in control of AI, and ask them to support an international agreement. Everything above the word “thank you” is optional.

    
What if I am not in the US, UK or EU?
      
Choose Elsewhere above. The message works anywhere, and almost every country has a way to look up your national representative online.

    
Will this take long?
      
The email is written for you, so it is a copy, a paste and a send. The phone call is about two minutes, most of which is waiting for someone to pick up.
